Spinal Impaction Device for Pedicle Screw Driver

Co-op
CTL — Medical Device Co-op
SLOTTED BOLT / TAPERED NUT — SECTION · replace with CAD render or photo

Designed a compact interference-fit mechanism to prevent unintended quick-connect release during surgical impaction — a slotted, slit-cut bolt mating with a tapered nut that induces radial deflection in the finger elements, specified in 17-4 PH stainless with 3D-printed proof-of-concept validation.

Built the full analytical chain end to end: axial load estimation from surgical counter-torque impaction, cantilever beam modeling of the slit-cut fingers, power-screw taper-to-clamping-force conversion, friction sufficiency check, and von Mises stress verification at the slit roots.
