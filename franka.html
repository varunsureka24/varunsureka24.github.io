Whiteboard Drawing with Franka Arm

Fall 2024 Best Project Python, Git, FrankaAPI
The Task

Program a 7-DOF Franka Emika Panda to autonomously pick up a pen, carry it to a whiteboard, draw lines and curves, and place the pen in a drop bin. The robot had to complete the full sequence three times while adapting to different pen locations, whiteboard positions, and orientations.

How It Works

Kinematics

  • Forward kinematics built from DH parameters
  • Inverse kinematics solved by gradient descent, which converges quickly and places the end effector accurately

Motion Planning

  • Joint-space and Cartesian-space interpolation for smooth, precise drawing motions
  • S-curve time parameterization for gradual acceleration and deceleration
  • Lines and arbitrary curves traced while the pen stays in contact with the board

Safety and Recovery

  • Collision avoidance around the table, pen holder, and whiteboard
  • Adaptive placement for varying pen and board positions
  • Human-in-the-loop input to recover from a failed grasp or misalignment
S-Curve Velocity Profile

A standard trapezoidal velocity profile has instantaneous jumps in acceleration where it changes segments. Those jumps raise the torque demand on the motors.

To fix that, I varied the time parameter passed to the interpolation function smoothly, so acceleration ramps up and down gradually. That keeps acceleration and jerk within feasible limits and makes the trajectory more stable.

My Role
  • Implemented the motion control system for continuous-line drawing on the whiteboard
  • Developed the velocity profiles and motion constraints for smooth, collision-free trajectories
